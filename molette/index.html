Your music.
One wheel.

Molette turns your iPhone into a pocket music player. Spin the wheel with your thumb, feel every notch click, and play the files you own.

A wheel you can feel

Every notch clicks, with a small vibration under your thumb. Press the centre and the album starts. Flip through your covers in a 3D Carousel, and turn the click off in Settings if you prefer silence.

Your files, on your phone

Copy your album folders from a Mac, a PC or the Files app. Molette sorts them by album and artist, with the artwork. You don't need an account or a connection, and there's no subscription and no ads.

FLAC and lossless

FLAC, ALAC, WAV and AIFF play alongside MP3, AAC and M4A. Albums play gapless, so a live record or a DJ mix runs from one track into the next without a break.

Locked screen, still playing

Background playback, lock screen and headphone controls, shuffle and repeat. Molette speaks 15 languages.

Questions

How do I play FLAC files on iPhone without a subscription?

Install Molette, copy your FLAC album folders into On My iPhone › Molette with the Files app or from a computer, and Molette plays them offline, gapless, with no account. The first 25 albums are free and Unlimited is a single payment.

How do I put my music on Molette?

On a computer, connect your iPhone, open Finder (or Apple Devices on Windows), go to Files and drop your album folders on Molette. On the iPhone, open the Files app and copy your folders into On My iPhone › Molette.

Which formats does Molette play?

MP3, AAC, M4A, ALAC, FLAC, WAV, AIFF and CAF. Albums play gapless. Molette uses the artwork in the file, or a JPG or PNG image from the album's folder.

Is Molette free?

Yes for your first 25 albums, with no time limit. Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It removes the album limit and adds Clean Up Names, seven colored cases and a Home Screen widget.

Does Molette need an internet connection or an account?

No. Molette has no account, no server and no ads, and it collects no data. Your music stays on your iPhone.

Does Molette keep playing in the background?

Yes. Music keeps playing with the screen locked, and you can pause, skip and go back from the lock screen, Control Center or your headphones.
