Privacy

Molette collects no data.

Updated November 2, 2026

Your music stays on your iPhone

Molette plays the files you put in its folder. Your songs, artwork and settings stay on your phone. Nothing leaves it.

No account needed

You don't sign up for anything, and the app has no server. There are no ads, analytics tools or third-party services in it.

Purchases

Apple handles the Unlimited purchase. I don't see your name, email or card. The details are in Apple's privacy policy.

Apple statistics

If you turned on sharing with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send me usage statistics and crash reports. They don't tell me who you are.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and the music and settings inside it go with it.
