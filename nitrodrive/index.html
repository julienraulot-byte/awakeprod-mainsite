How a race goes

  • Hit them at speed.They wreck.

    Six cars on the road and five of them after you. Rockets, bombs, oil and spikes come back at you. Crash yourself and you still get a second to steer your own wreck into the pack. That counts too.

  • Hold to drift.Lift to fire.

    Slide to steer, the throttle is automatic. Hold your thumb into a tight bend to drift and charge a turbo, lift to fire it. One thumb, the whole race.

  • Double tap: boost.Triple tap: NITRO.

    Boost when the gauge is a third full, NITRO MODE when it is full. A short tap uses the item you picked up. A race lasts about two minutes.

  • 20 circuits.Day and night.

    Night rain in NEON, the seafront of RIVIERA, CANYON at sunset, a toy room with loops, a station in orbit. Three laps each.

Questions.

How much does NITRODRIVE cost?

License C is free: four circuits, their cup, time trials and free runs. One purchase of 4.99 USD unlocks licenses B, A and S for good: 12 more circuits right away, and the cars, the PRO cup and its 4 circuits as you earn stars. Nothing else to buy, ever. No subscription. Family Sharing included.

Does NITRODRIVE work offline?

Yes. Races, cups, time trials and free runs work with no connection. Only the world boards need one.

How do the controls work?

One thumb. Slide left or right to steer; the throttle is automatic. Hold your thumb into a tight bend to drift and charge a turbo, lift to fire it. Double tap to boost when the gauge is a third full, triple tap for NITRO when it is full. A short tap uses the item you picked up.

How do takedowns work?

Push a rival into a wall or into traffic and it wrecks: that is a takedown. Rivals hunt you back with rockets, bombs, oil and spikes. If you crash, you still get a second to steer your own wreck into the pack, and that counts too.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The world boards only know a nickname and a random id created by the game.
