Hold to drift

Your thumb slides to steer. The throttle is automatic. Everything else is one of these four moves.

Hold into a tight bend and the car drifts. The longer you hold, the bigger the turbo you charge.

How a takedown goes

First you catch a rival.

Then you lean on him.

Then the wall does the rest.

Then you keep the throttle open.

Rivals hunt you back with rockets, bombs, oil and spikes. If they get you, you have one second to steer your own wreck into them.

Questions

How much does NITRODRIVE cost?

License C is free with no time limit: four circuits, a cup, time trials, free runs and the daily challenge. One in-app purchase of 4.99 USD unlocks everything else for good: licenses B, A and S, the PRO cup, every circuit and every car. There is no subscription.

Does NITRODRIVE work offline?

Yes. Races, cups, time trials and free runs work with no connection. Only the world boards and the daily challenge board need one.

How do the controls work?

One thumb. Slide left or right to steer; the throttle is automatic. Hold your thumb into a tight bend to drift and charge a turbo, lift to fire it. Double tap to boost when the gauge is a third full, triple tap for NITRO when it is full. A short tap uses the item you picked up.

How do takedowns work?

Push a rival into a wall or into traffic and it wrecks: that is a takedown. Rivals hunt you back with rockets, bombs, oil and spikes. If you crash, you can still steer your wreck into a rival for one second.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, no ads and no analytics. The world boards only know a nickname and a random id created by the game.

Which languages does NITRODRIVE speak?

English and French.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. NITRODRIVE launches on iPhone first; Android comes later.

Does the purchase work with Family Sharing?

Yes. The full game is one non-consumable purchase with Family Sharing on, so your family group shares it.
