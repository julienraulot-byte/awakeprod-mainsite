Flight

Dogfights down in the valleys

Your right thumb flies the jet from anywhere on the glass. Your left works the guns. Hug the river, slip under the ridge, climb through the pass with a fighter on your tail.

  • Auto gunThe cannon fires when a target crosses the sight. Inside 250 m it hits twice as hard.
  • BreakA missile on your six? Roll hard and it overshoots.
  • GrazeRounds that miss by a hair charge your EMP.

Missiles

Lock four. Fire once.

Hold MSL and sweep the circle across the sky. Every contact it touches gets a lock, and the tone climbs with each one. Let go and the whole salvo leaves the rails, one missile per lock, the score multiplied by the count.

  • RocketsA pod burst for boats, trucks and gun sites.
  • BombsDrop on the marker for the targets on the ground.
  • EMPFill the meter, tap EMP, and the pulse clears the sky around you. Let it fill all the way and it fires on its own when a hit would bring you down.

FAQ

Questions.

How do the controls work?

Two thumbs, portrait. Put your right thumb down anywhere on the screen and it becomes the stick. The left thumb has the keys: GUN, MSL, AB for the afterburner, the two dodge keys, FACE to swing onto a target, BONUS and EMP. On night missions an NVG key turns on night vision. The cannon also fires on its own when a target sits in the sight.

What is free?

Missions 1 to 3 (the Alpine Front, boss included) and Free Run in the Alpine Valley. One purchase unlocks the Full Game: the other nine missions and the four other territories. Restore it on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID.

Does Redsky work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays with no connection. Game Center scores are sent when you are back online and signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no ads and no analytics. Game Center is optional: if you are signed in, your best scores go to its boards.
